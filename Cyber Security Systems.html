1. Overview of Cyber Security

Cyber Security refers to the protection of computer systems, networks, and data from unauthorized access, attacks, or damage. It plays a critical role in safeguarding personal, organizational, and national information infrastructure. With the rapid growth of the internet and digital services, cyber security systems help prevent data breaches, financial loss, and misuse of sensitive information.

Cyber Security is essential in today’s digital world. It protects sensitive data from cyber attacks and unauthorized access.

“Cyber security is much more than a matter of IT.”

2. Major Cyber Security Systems

Cyber security systems work together to protect computers, networks, and data from various cyber threats. Below is a list of the most commonly used cyber security systems.

  • Firewall Protection Systems
  • Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS)
  • Intrusion Prevention Systems (IPS)
  • Anti-virus and Anti-malware Systems
  • Encryption and Data Protection Systems

3. Explanation of Cyber Security Systems

Firewall Protection Systems

Firewalls act as a barrier between trusted internal networks and untrusted external networks. They monitor network traffic and block unauthorized access while allowing legitimate data to pass through safely.

Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS)

Intrusion Detection Systems continuously analyze network activity to detect suspicious behavior, potential attacks, or policy violations. IDS mainly provides alerts to system administrators for further action.

Intrusion Prevention Systems (IPS)

Intrusion Prevention Systems not only detect cyber threats but also take immediate action to prevent attacks. IPS blocks malicious traffic automatically, helping reduce damage in real time.

Anti-virus and Anti-malware Systems

Anti-virus and anti-malware systems scan computers and networks to identify harmful software such as viruses, worms, spyware, and trojans. These systems help remove or quarantine malicious files to keep systems secure.

Encryption and Data Protection Systems

Encryption systems convert sensitive information into unreadable formats to protect it from unauthorized access. Data protection systems ensure confidentiality, integrity, and secure transmission of information.
